# Reader Roles

What each common reader decides, wants to know first, and usually doesn't need. Use this to choose what leads and how the email's opening and closing are framed. It never adds facts: if a reader would want something the source doesn't give, leave it out; if the email asks the reader to do or meet something the source doesn't explain, list it under "Not in the source."

Preferences in the attorney's reader context override these defaults. Firms can edit this file to match their own clients.

## CEO
- **Decides:** strategy, priorities, major risks, who owns the response.
- **Wants first:** does this affect the business, and does anything need my decision?
- **Emphasize:** business impact, reputational and strategic risk, decisions needing sign-off.
- **Usually skip:** procedural mechanics and legal reasoning.
- **Often loops in:** General Counsel; CFO when money is involved.

## CFO
- **Decides:** budgets, financial exposure, reserves, spending on compliance.
- **Wants first:** what could this cost, and when?
- **Emphasize:** penalties, fees, settlement amounts, deadlines tied to spending.
- **Usually skip:** legal analysis beyond what drives the numbers.
- **Often loops in:** General Counsel.

## General Counsel
- **Decides:** legal risk, compliance obligations, escalation, outside counsel.
- **Wants first:** what's required, by when, and what's still uncertain?
- **Emphasize:** obligations, scope limits, deadlines, open legal questions. Can take more legal precision than other readers.
- **Usually skip:** basic explanations of legal terms.
- **Often loops in:** CEO; compliance or HR, depending on the topic.

## HR lead
- **Decides:** workplace policies, training, manager guidance, accommodations.
- **Wants first:** what changes for employees and managers?
- **Emphasize:** policy updates, training needs, manager conduct.
- **Usually skip:** enforcement history and financial detail.
- **Often loops in:** General Counsel.

## Chief Compliance Officer
- **Decides:** compliance programs, controls, monitoring, regulator relationships.
- **Wants first:** what must the program do differently, and by when?
- **Emphasize:** required procedures, documentation, exam or audit exposure.
- **Usually skip:** strategic framing.
- **Often loops in:** General Counsel.

## Chief Information Security Officer
- **Decides:** security controls, incident response, vendor security.
- **Wants first:** what technical or incident-response changes are required?
- **Emphasize:** notification clocks, vendor obligations, security standards.
- **Usually skip:** legal background.
- **Often loops in:** General Counsel.

## Other roles
Focus on the decisions that person actually makes. If you can't tell what those are, ask the attorney one short question.
