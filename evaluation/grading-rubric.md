# Grading rubric

The rules every draft was graded against. The grader saw the source, the draft, and the case's expectations, but not which version wrote the draft.

## Expectation types

| Type | Fail it when | How strict |
|---|---|---|
| `[error]` | The draft does the thing even once: a wrong date, an invented fact, an overstated claim, an allegation stated as fact, a misquote | Zero tolerance. Quote the offending sentence. |
| `[coverage]` | The point is missing, or present with a different meaning | Judge meaning, not wording. Equivalent wording passes. |
| `[flag]` | The attorney isn't told about the item anywhere a reviewing attorney would look | A draft with no review notes fails these. |
| `[format]` | The output template isn't followed | The exact wording or heading named. Expected to fail for drafts written without the skill. |
| `[noise]` | Any email sentence fails the email gate, or any attorney item fails the attorney gate | List each failing sentence or item. |

**Quotes:** every quote the draft attributes to the source must appear in the source word for word. A script checks the Sources table; the grader checks any other quotes by hand. A changed first capital or apostrophe style isn't a misquote.

## The two gates

- **Email gate:** a sentence passes only if, without it, the client would misunderstand what happened, not know whether it applies, miss a deadline, or not know what to do. Restatements, background, and history fail.
- **Attorney gate:** an item passes only if you can honestly finish this sentence: "Without this, the attorney might send something wrong, or miss a decision, because…". Routine items fail. A section with no items passes; a draft with no review notes at all fails.

**Protected content always passes the email gate:** who's covered, deadlines, amounts that drive decisions, required actions, hedges and qualifiers, and "no outcome yet" statements. A "check for developments" item for a source more than three months old always passes the attorney gate.

## Scoring

- **Error-free draft:** no `[error]` item failed.
- **Key points covered:** `[coverage]` items passed, as a share of all coverage items.
- **Risks flagged:** `[flag]` items passed, as a share of all flag items.
- **Email length:** words in the email part of the draft. Drafts written without the skill have no separate email, so their whole reply is counted.
